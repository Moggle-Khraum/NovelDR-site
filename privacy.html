Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

NovelDR is built to keep your reading private. Your library, downloaded novels, reading progress and settings stay on your device. The app does send a small amount of data to two third-party services, described below. This policy explains what they receive and why we use them.

1. Information We Collect

Your reading data is not collected. Your library, reading history, downloaded novels and personal information are never sent to us or to any third party.

Two third-party services receive limited data from the app:

  • Crash reports (Sentry): if the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry. It may include the error message, the app version, and basic device information such as the operating system and device model.
  • Online counter (Cloudflare Workers): while the app is open, it sends a simple "someone is online" ping. Like any web request, the ping reaches Cloudflare with your IP address attached. The developer receives only the ping and uses it only to count how many people are using NovelDR.

2. Data Storage

All downloaded novels, reading progress, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data.

3. Permissions

NovelDR may request storage permission only to save downloaded novels to your device. The app also needs network access to send the crash reports and online counter signals described above.

4. Third-Party Services

NovelDR uses two third-party services, listed below with the reason for each.

Sentry (crash reporting)

Why we use it: Sentry was added in version 3.8.60 (July 2026), because the app was crashing often and unpredictably, sometimes with no clear error. Sentry helps us catch and diagnose a handful of these errors. It is used for debugging, not for tracking users. Sentry's privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

Cloudflare online counter

Why we use it: The counter answers one question: how many people use NovelDR. Nothing more. The developer sees only the count from the ping, and no library, reading, or personal information. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

5. Contact

If you have privacy concerns, please contact us via GitHub.
